What I work on

ML when data is scarce

My PhD research: building NLP models for niche educational tasks, like judging how well a student understood a text or how good a paraphrase is, where labelled data is scarce and you can't just throw more examples at the problem. Transfer learning, careful fine-tuning and LLMs do the heavy lifting.

transfer learningfine-tuningLLMseducational NLP

Quantization & sparsity

Making big models small: taking LLMs down to INT8, INT4 and even ternary weights, and pruning the weights that don't pull their weight, structured and unstructured, so models fit on edge hardware without losing accuracy.
